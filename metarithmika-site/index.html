Guarantees and questions

Each guarantee has written conditions in our terms of engagement.

Read the client guarantee

What if you make an error in my books?

We correct it at no cost if you report it in the 30 days after the report or filing. Government penalties are not covered.

Can I cancel my plan?

Yes. Give written notice 10 days before your next billing date. There is no long-term contract.

What if I am not happy with the service?

Tell us in your first 30 days. If we cannot fix it, cancel and we refund your first month's fee.

Which accounting software do you use?

QuickBooks Online or Sage 50, with receipt capture, and payroll through Wagepoint or your current provider.

Do you keep books for non-profits?

Yes: grant tracking against your funder's budget, board-ready reports and T3010-ready records.
